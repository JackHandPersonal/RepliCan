Player pain and death voice lines
=================================

These are recordings of a person, not synthesised speech. Both packs are CC0 (public domain
dedication): free for any use, commercial included, with no attribution required and no licence
text that has to ship with the game. The credit below is courtesy, not obligation.

PlayerGruntsMale/     "grunts of male death and pain", OpenGameArt, CC0
                      https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain
                      One 49.7 s take, 44.1 kHz, cut into 3 death cries and 16 hurt grunts.

PlayerGruntsFemale/   "female hurt grunts and groans", OpenGameArt, CC0
                      https://opengameart.org/content/female-hurt-grunts-groans
                      One 13.0 s Ogg, 48 kHz, cut into 2 death cries and 5 hurt grunts.

Both were cut on silence and level-matched by Tools/import_voice_grunts.py, which can be re-run to
reproduce every file here from the two downloads.

_RetiredSynth/        the earlier Piper-synthesised grunts, kept only for comparison. The game does
                      not read this folder: PlayVoiceBark globs one directory, without recursing.
